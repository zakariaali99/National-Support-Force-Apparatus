## 🎨 Design Philosophy & Target System Identity
- **System Focus**: **Administrative HR Management & Data-Entry System** (NOT a military operational combat system). Focus on fast, frictionless record entry, clean data tables, smooth form dialogs, and clear administrative management workflows.
- **Visual Aesthetic**:
  - Crisp, bright, modern enterprise light mode (`#ffffff` background, `#f8fafc` subtle slate tint, `#0a2540` deep navy typography).
  - Clean Cairo typography with explicit type scale (`text-display`, `text-title`, `text-section`, `text-body`, `text-body-sm`, `text-label`, `text-caption`) and weights (400, 600, 700).
  - High data-density executive tables with inline row action toolbars (`تعديل`, `ملاحظة`, `مهمة`, `طباعة`).
  - Full Arabic localization with **zero technical English slug/code input fields** shown to administrative users (auto-generate internal keys under the hood).
  - Slim collapsible sidebar + intuitive RTL Arabic pagination (`السابق` / `التالي`).
